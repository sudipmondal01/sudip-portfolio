The Storyteller

Why I shoot
the way I do.

Light in Kolkata changes three times before noon.

Growing up here taught me that the most powerful frame isn't the one you plan — it's the one you're ready for when the world tilts in a direction you didn't expect. I became a photographer because I couldn't stop noticing things other people walked past.

I don't shoot events. I shoot the space between moments — the breath before the vows, the glance across the table, the stillness after the music stops. That's the frame that lasts.
