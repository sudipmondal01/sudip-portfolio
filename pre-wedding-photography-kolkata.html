Popular Locations

Where in Kolkata
we can shoot.

Kolkata is one of India's most cinematic cities — every street, every ghat, every rooftop holds a frame worth capturing.

Victoria Memorial

Iconic heritage architecture with sweeping greens — perfect for elegant, timeless portraits.

Prinsep Ghat

The Hooghly riverside at golden hour creates a warm, painterly backdrop unlike anywhere else.

Howrah Bridge

Dramatic industrial structure — best at dusk when the city lights reflect on the river.

Heritage Lanes, North Kolkata

Crumbling colonial architecture, terracotta temples, and narrow gullies — a storyteller's dream.

Botanical Gardens

Lush greenery and the famous Great Banyan Tree — natural, soft-light portraits with depth.

Your Home / Rooftop

Intimate in-home sessions often produce the most emotionally real images — no travel required.

FAQ

Pre-Wedding Shoot FAQ

A pre-wedding shoot is a dedicated portrait session before your wedding day — at a location meaningful to you. It produces cinematic images used for invitations, décor, and keepsakes, and also helps you feel relaxed in front of the camera on your actual wedding day.

Victoria Memorial, Prinsep Ghat, Howrah Bridge, heritage lanes in North Kolkata, the Botanical Gardens, and rooftops are all popular. I also scout lesser-known spots for couples who want something unique.

A standard session is 2–3 hours. For multiple locations we plan 4–5 hours. Golden hour (the last hour before sunset) is always included as the primary shooting window.

Yes — Goa, Rajasthan, Himachal, Kerala, and the North-East are all popular destinations. Travel packages are available and customised per location.
